## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** tierra viva, oficio serio, ciencia sin bata. References: a soil-science field guide with colour plates; the light of a coffee farm at 7am; a well-printed seed catalogue.
2. **Journey (authored):** the visitor lands on the surface (hands sowing) → learns who makes this and how → sees the three products → picks the need of their own crop → sees the crops it is used in → descends through the soil profile (the process) → meets the people in the field → the seed they "sowed" at the top germinates beside the WhatsApp button.
3. **Energy:** calm open, steady middle, the loudest moment is the descent (process), quiet warmth after it, confident close.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage:** see the curve below. The one moment: the descent through the soil strata.
5. **What no other site does:** the scroll *sows*. A single orange seed (from the logo) falls down a depth gauge as you read, and germinates into a sprout at the end, next to the action.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal:** editorial-natural. Premium, not minimal: real colour regions, real photography, warm materials.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes (strata). Not a continuous flight.
8. **Assets:** only the client's own homepage images (12) and two YouTube videos. No generation this round.

## Grammar: Estratos (soil-profile descent) — new grammar

- **Unit:** a stratum. Each section is a layer with its own hard ground; grounds get darker and warmer as you descend (paper → leaf → sand → humus), then the close resurfaces to forest green (the harvest).
- **Nav:** a sticky material header (wordmark, sections, one action) **plus a depth gauge** on the right edge on desktop: the strata listed top to bottom, clickable, with the seed marking where you are.
- **Hero:** the surface. A pinned layered scene (photo plate, scrim, type, seed planes at three depths) that settles into a rounded plate as you leave it.
- **Close:** germination. The seed reaches the bottom of the gauge, a sprout draws itself next to the CTA, and the CTA carries the visitor's own selection from "Su cultivo".
- **Bans:** gradient drift between grounds (hard cuts only), scrub video, centred copy in more than one act, counters (no real figures beyond "desde 2013"), magnet/spotlight, eyebrows, "scroll" cues.
