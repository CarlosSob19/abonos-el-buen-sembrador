# Surface brief — Inicio (index.html)

Mode: **Persuade**. Redesign: the Kolau template look is replaced; content, products, prices, store links and contact channels are preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Soil is the protagonist. The page reads top-down like a soil profile — open sky and seedling at the surface, dark living humus where the science lives, warm sand where the farmer decides. It refuses the agro-template of a green banner over a grid of identical product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Forest green #173F32 owns whole regions (process band, footer); warm white #FAFAF6 ground; sand #EDE6D8 for decision surfaces; humus brown #3B2A1E for the science band; the logo's sowing orange #E8892A only on seeds and the primary action. Bricolage Grotesque display (light, tight), Hanken Grotesk text. Large 40px radii on photographic plates, hairline rules, no shadows beyond one soft lift.

STORY: Understand what they make (organic, mature, biotech compost) → see the three real products with price → find the product by crop need → trust the process and the people → write on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: h1 “Insumos agrícolas con biotecnología, hechos en Antioquia desde 2013.” in large light display; one sentence; primary orange WhatsApp button + “Ver productos”. Right: hero seedling photo in a tall rounded plate, a trail of orange seed-dots falling from the logo’s hand motif into the soil. Sticky compact header above.

FORM: Code-led editorial single page; signature move = falling seed-dots (from the logo) that settle on load and mark section transitions. No seed key (decision round skipped at the client's explicit request).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
